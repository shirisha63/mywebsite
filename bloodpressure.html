🥗 Food Guidance

Food suggestions are separated according to the BP category.

🟠 Foods for LOW BP

  • Banana
  • Milk and yogurt
  • Dates and raisins
  • Eggs
  • Chicken
  • Soups and fluids

🟢 Foods for HIGH BP

  • Leafy green vegetables
  • Oats
  • Orange and other fruits
  • Unsalted nuts
  • Beans and lentils
  • Low-sodium meals
⚠️ Important: This website is intended for BP tracking and educational food guidance. It does not measure blood pressure by itself and should not replace a validated blood-pressure monitor or professional medical advice.
